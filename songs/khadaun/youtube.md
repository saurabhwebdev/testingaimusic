Sinhasan pe Ram ki khadaun… aur neeche zameen pe baitha Bharat.

Chaudah baras Bharat ji ne raja hote hue bhi ek vanvasi ki tarah bitaaye. Unhone pran liya tha ki agar Ram ek din bhi der se aaye, toh woh apni chita khud jala denge. Ye gaana unhi chaudah barson ki pratiksha hai — aur us aakhri bhor ki, jab hawa ne kaan mein kaha, "woh aa raha hai."

Chorus mein saath gaaiye — Ram Ram… Siya Ram. 🙏
